## Required integration and checks

1. Build a versioned, span-level intake index for the entire attachment, retaining source spans and marking individual concepts and relationships as mapped, partial, unmapped, or intentionally excluded with reasons. Do not copy private source material into the public repository without need.
2. Resolve concepts into a graph that permits arbitrary components, physical systems, people and organizations: primitive → engine → subsystem → product or operational workflow → responsible actors → quality obligations → observable evidence. Relations can be many-to-many and need not follow one fixed stack.
3. Select diverse, prospectively documented pilots such as numerical geometry to mapping, transforms to codec operation, payments to reconciliation and support, and drivers to timing and recovery. For each, inspect primary sources and real failures, specify applicability and counterexamples, and have an independent reviewer challenge the path and its evidence. These examples do not delimit scope.
4. Separate source-stated claims, independently verified findings, hypotheses and unresearched leads. Track the date, source version, inaccessible material and contradictory evidence. Do not infer research completion from a category label.
5. Reconcile this work with the separate role list when supplied, without waiting to research the roles and workflows already visible in the Atlas. Review the shared conversation separately if a complete read of that source is still required.
