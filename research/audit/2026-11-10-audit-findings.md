## Material findings

1. **FP-08 missed a real linked fix.** Reanimated issue #4422 links merged PR #4776, whose summary says it fixes the Android freeze and whose maintainer reports testing. The previous card said no fix was established. It has been corrected with explicit project-reported and not independently reproduced status: https://github.com/software-mansion/react-native-reanimated/pull/4776 .
2. **The earlier correction of “10 debloating tools” was itself incomplete.** The study obtained source for 24 tools, built/ran 17, then evaluated 10; both ten years of work and ten evaluated tools are true at different points. The original empirical note's 10-tool evaluation claim was supported. The later log's implication that the phrase was necessarily wrong conflated stages; benchmark and bloat notes now state all three stages. Primary paper: https://arxiv.org/html/2312.13274v2 .
3. **FP-18 has conflicting CI history.** The issue says the all-packages build was not run in ordinary CI; its later PR says build already ran in the lint job and only metadata validation was missing. Both agree validation was missing. The card now flags the disagreement; exact historical CI configuration remains to be checked. https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/issues/7397 and https://github.com/pydantic/pydantic-ai/pull/7414 .
4. **FP-04's retry mitigation was too broadly phrased.** Its direct Python `tools.execute` / `tools.proxy` mitigation should not be generalized to session or TypeScript paths. Later issue discussion describes additional risk; it does not establish a reproduced duplicate email. The card is clarified: https://github.com/ComposioHQ/composio/issues/3654 .
5. **Prior structural checks missed compatibility fields.** 20 planned cells have a legacy cell/run pair absent from the run-link table; twelve lack an exact ledger ID, and none has another run-link row. One of the 20 (PC75) uses an ID linked to a different cell. Several missing-ID calls and outputs were found in raw history, so missing links are not proof the work was not done. Some may be aliases or references to batch runs. They require reconciliation, not deletion: see `record-integrity.md`.
6. **Three retrospective extraction records have raw event evidence.** The app history tool's size-limit failure did not mean source records were absent. Retained JSONL events provide timestamps, five/four/five URL lists and fetch search hints. The hints were arguments to extraction calls, not proven original search queries. See `session-inventory.md`.
7. **Other observations:** the checked corpus counts for build studies, LibCache, SWE-CC and test-oracle study occur in the source papers, subject to their sample/construct limitations. The React Native Web, Redis and VueUse source narratives support the narrow findings sampled earlier. RepoPulse's live baseline could not be opened through the parent's web route; its no-score rule is `cannot-assess` in this audit. The Criticality Score maintainer announced a shutdown date and a July 2025 last successful run; the announcement alone does not independently verify infrastructure shutdown.
